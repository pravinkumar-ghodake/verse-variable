From the Word to the Wave

The words name two dimensions of the journal's identity: the literary expressions at its heart, and the wider intellectual perspective the editor brings to reading them. That perspective includes science, but it does not prescribe what a literary work must mean or where a reflection must lead.

ABOUT THE JOURNAL

Literature, Read on Its Own Terms

Verse & Variable is an independent Indian literary journal devoted to poetry, shayari, ghazals and other literary expressions across languages. Each work is approached through its own language, imagery, cultural setting and emotional movement.

Edited by Pravinkumar Ghodake, an independent researcher in theoretical, computational and experimental mechanics, the journal is shaped by a wider intellectual curiosity spanning science, literature, philosophy, books, music, cinema, sport and lived experience. These interests inform the editor's perspective; they do not prescribe what a poem must mean or how it must be read.
