THE CENTRAL IDEA

From the Word to the Wave

The words name two dimensions of the journal's identity: the literary expressions at its heart, and the wider intellectual perspective the editor brings to reading them. That perspective includes science, but it does not prescribe what a literary work must mean or where a reflection must lead.

ABOUT THE JOURNAL

Beyond the Words. Beneath the Waves.

Verse & Variable is a personal intellectual exploration of literature, humanity and science. It brings together a multilingual Indian literary archive and reflections informed by a continuing engagement with theoretical, computational and experimental mechanics.

Some expressions lead towards relationships, memory and identity. Others invite philosophical questions or connections with nonlinear dynamics, nonlinear optics, wave propagation and the complexity of physical systems.
