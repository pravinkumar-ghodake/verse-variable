ABOUT THE JOURNAL

Beyond the Words. Beneath the Waves.

Verse & Variable is a personal intellectual exploration of literature, humanity and science. It brings together a multilingual Indian literary archive and reflections informed by a continuing engagement with theoretical, computational and experimental mechanics.

Some expressions lead towards relationships, memory and identity. Others invite philosophical questions or connections with nonlinear dynamics, wave propagation and the complexity of physical systems.
