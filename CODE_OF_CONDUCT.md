## 1. Our Commitment

Verse & Variable is an independent literary and scientific publication that brings together diverse literary traditions, philosophical perspectives and scientific disciplines.

We are committed to maintaining an environment in which intellectual curiosity, cultural appreciation, thoughtful disagreement and independent expression can coexist.

Participation should be guided by mutual respect, intellectual honesty and consideration for others.

## 2. Our Principles

We value:

- Respect for linguistic and cultural diversity.
- Appreciation of literary traditions and their historical contexts.
- Intellectual independence.
- Evidence-based scientific discussion.
- Constructive criticism.
- Freedom of interpretation.
- Accurate attribution and acknowledgment of creators.
- Openness to different perspectives.
- Responsible and respectful communication.

## 3. Expected Behaviour

Participants are encouraged to:

- Engage with ideas thoughtfully and constructively.
- Respect differences in language, culture and literary interpretation.
- Criticise arguments rather than attack individuals.
- Support factual and scientific claims with appropriate evidence.
- Acknowledge the work and intellectual contributions of others.
- Correct errors respectfully.
- Recognise uncertainty where evidence is incomplete.
- Participate in discussions with patience and intellectual humility.

## 4. Unacceptable Behaviour

The following conduct is inconsistent with the values of this publication:

- Harassment, intimidation or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory or hateful language.
- Deliberate misrepresentation of another person's work.
- Knowingly false attribution of literary or scientific material.
- Plagiarism or unauthorised appropriation of intellectual work.
- Threats, stalking or targeted abuse.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Deliberate disruption of the publication or its community.
- Repeated bad-faith conduct intended to prevent meaningful discussion.

## 5. Cultural and Linguistic Sensitivity

Verse & Variable engages with literary traditions in several Indian languages and the wider South Asian cultural landscape.

No language or literary tradition should be treated as inherently superior or inferior.

Participants should recognise that literary expressions may carry historical, cultural, philosophical and emotional meanings that are not always apparent through literal translation.

Differences in interpretation are welcome when expressed respectfully.

## 6. Intellectual Disagreement

Disagreement is an important part of literary and scientific inquiry.

Participants may question interpretations, challenge assumptions, dispute scientific explanations and propose alternative readings.

Such disagreement should remain focused on reasoning, evidence and interpretation.

No participant should be expected to agree with an editorial position merely to participate.

## 7. Scientific Integrity

Scientific discussions should distinguish:

- Established scientific findings.
- Mathematical or theoretical models.
- Experimental observations.
- Interpretations and analogies.
- Speculative hypotheses.

Scientific terminology should not be used to give unsupported authority to literary interpretations.

Equally, literary and philosophical expression should not be dismissed merely because it does not conform to scientific methods.

## 8. Copyright and Attribution

Participants must respect the rights of authors, poets, lyricists, composers, translators, researchers and other creators.

Attribution should be accurate.

Where the source of an expression is uncertain, uncertainty should be acknowledged.

Permission should be obtained where required before reproducing protected third-party material.

## 9. Editorial Responsibility

Verse & Variable is curated by its founder and editor, Pravinkumar Ghodake.

The editor is responsible for publication decisions, editorial standards and the final presentation of material.

Suggestions, contributions and disagreements are welcome, but submission does not guarantee publication.

## 10. Reporting Concerns

Concerns about harassment, plagiarism, misattribution, inappropriate conduct or other violations of this code may be communicated through the founder's official website:

https://sites.google.com/view/pravinkumarghodake

Reports should include sufficient information to understand the concern.

Please avoid publishing confidential or sensitive personal information in public discussions.

## 11. Enforcement

Reported concerns will be considered in light of their nature, available information and potential impact.

Depending on the circumstances, responses may include:

- Clarification or correction.
- Removal of inappropriate material.
- Rejection of a contribution.
- Restriction of participation in publication-related discussions.
- Other reasonable editorial or administrative action.

Decisions will be guided by fairness, proportionality and the interests of the publication.

## 12. Scope

This code applies to participation in publication-related discussions, contributions, repository interactions and other activities directly associated with Verse & Variable.

It does not restrict legitimate criticism, scholarly disagreement or respectful debate.

## 13. Closing Principle

Literature invites us to understand experiences beyond our own.

Science invites us to question what we believe we understand.

Both require openness, intellectual honesty and respect.

Verse & Variable seeks to preserve these values.
