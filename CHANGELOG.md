
# Changelog

**Verse & Variable**  
*A line of verse. A line of inquiry.*  
**لفظ سے لہر تک — Lafz se Lehar tak**

All significant changes to Verse & Variable are documented in this file.

This changelog records notable developments in the publication's editorial direction, literary archive, technical infrastructure, accessibility, design, documentation, and scientific content.

It is intended to provide readers, contributors, and future maintainers with a transparent record of the publication's development.

Routine corrections, minor formatting adjustments, and individual article additions do not necessarily require separate changelog entries. Significant editorial, structural, or technical changes should be recorded.

The format is inspired by [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/).

## Change Categories

Changes may be organised using the following categories:

- **Added** — New features, content collections, documentation, or capabilities.
- **Changed** — Modifications to existing features, editorial practices, or design.
- **Improved** — Enhancements to usability, accessibility, performance, or content quality.
- **Fixed** — Corrections to technical, editorial, bibliographic, or presentation errors.
- **Removed** — Features, content, or practices discontinued from the publication.
- **Security** — Changes addressing security issues or vulnerabilities.

## [Unreleased]

### Added

- Established the foundational identity of Verse & Variable as an independent multilingual literary and scientific publication.
- Introduced the publication's central identity: *A line of verse. A line of inquiry.*
- Established the Urdu expression **لفظ سے لہر تک — Lafz se Lehar tak** as part of the publication's identity.
- Created the Jekyll-based publication structure for GitHub Pages.
- Established separate collections for individual literary explorations and weekly collections.
- Introduced reusable layouts and components for literary entries, metadata, navigation, and visual presentation.
- Established the initial multilingual framework for Urdu, Hindi, Marathi, Kannada, Tamil, Malayalam, and Telugu.
- Introduced the publication's visual identity, typography system, responsive design, and wavefield-inspired visual elements.
- Added foundational discovery and publication resources, including sitemap, RSS feed, `robots.txt`, `llms.txt`, `llms-full.txt`, and `humans.txt`.
- Established documentation covering contribution practices, editorial standards, copyright, privacy, accessibility, security, and acceptable community conduct.
- Added a publication disclaimer distinguishing literary interpretation, scientific explanation, and practical technical advice.
- Established a dedicated changelog for recording significant future developments.

### Changed

- Established a consistent editorial framework for presenting original literary material, transliteration, translation, attribution, and commentary.
- Defined the intended four-paragraph structure for individual literary explorations.
- Established the principle that scientific connections must be intellectually meaningful and must not be forced onto literary material.

### Improved

- Established accessibility objectives referencing WCAG 2.2 Level AA.
- Defined multilingual presentation considerations, including right-to-left Urdu typography and regional-script rendering.
- Introduced principles for distinguishing literary interpretation, scientific analogy, theoretical explanation, numerical simulation, and experimentally validated results.

## How Future Updates Will Be Recorded

Future entries should use the following structure:

### [Version or Unreleased]

#### Added

- Describe the new feature, content collection, or publication capability.

#### Changed

- Describe a significant modification to existing content or functionality.

#### Improved

- Explain improvements to accessibility, performance, usability, or editorial quality.

#### Fixed

- Identify an issue and briefly describe its correction.

#### Removed

- Document the removal of an existing feature or practice.

#### Security

- Document relevant security-related changes without disclosing sensitive vulnerability details.

## Versioning Principles

Verse & Variable is primarily a literary and intellectual publication rather than a conventional software product.

Version numbers may be introduced when significant milestones justify them.

Not every article, weekly collection, correction, or small design adjustment requires a new version number.

The changelog should prioritise clarity, historical accuracy, and meaningful developments over the accumulation of unnecessary version labels.

## Editorial and Technical History

The changelog should be maintained alongside the publication.

Entries should be factual, reasonably concise, and understandable without requiring readers to inspect the repository's entire commit history.

Where an update substantially changes an existing editorial policy, publication structure, or technical capability, the relevant change should be documented here.
