## 1. An Archive Built on Shared Intellectual Heritage

Verse & Variable is an independent publication shaped by the accumulated intellectual, literary, artistic, and scientific contributions of generations of creators and thinkers.

No literary or scientific archive exists in isolation. Every meaningful exploration draws, in some way, upon languages, cultural traditions, historical records, published scholarship, creative expression, and the work of people who preserve and develop knowledge.

This page recognises those wider contributions and establishes the principles through which acknowledgements will be maintained.

## 2. Literary Traditions and Their Creators

Our deepest acknowledgement belongs to the writers, poets, lyricists, playwrights, storytellers, philosophers, composers, and other creators whose work makes literary exploration possible.

We recognise the richness and diversity of:

- Urdu poetry, ghazals, nazms, and shayari.
- Hindi literature and poetry.
- Marathi literary and poetic traditions.
- Kannada literature and poetry.
- Tamil literary traditions.
- Malayalam literature and poetry.
- Telugu literature and poetry.
- Other Indian and South Asian literary expressions.
- International literary traditions that meaningfully contribute to the archive.

These traditions are not merely sources of quotations. They represent languages, histories, artistic innovations, philosophical perspectives, cultural memories, and deeply personal forms of human expression.

The archive seeks to approach them with respect for their original contexts and distinctive literary identities.

## 3. Writers, Translators, Performers, and Contributors

Literary works often reach readers through the efforts of multiple people.

Depending on the nature of a work, its acknowledgement may recognise:

- Original writers and poets.
- Lyricists and playwrights.
- Composers and musicians.
- Singers and performers.
- Translators and adapters.
- Editors and commentators.
- Archivists and researchers.
- Publishers and literary institutions.

These roles should not be confused or combined without justification.

A singer should not automatically be credited as the lyricist. A translator should not be presented as the original poet. An adaptation should not be described as an exact reproduction of the source.

Where reliable information is available, acknowledgements should distinguish these contributions accurately.

## 4. Libraries, Archives, and Cultural Institutions

We recognise the importance of institutions and independent initiatives that preserve, catalogue, document, and make literary and historical material available.

These may include:

- Public and university libraries.
- National and regional archives.
- Digital literary repositories.
- Cultural and linguistic institutions.
- Museums and heritage organisations.
- Scholarly databases.
- Research and documentation centres.
- Independent preservation initiatives.

Their cataloguing, conservation, digitisation, and documentation efforts help readers discover works that might otherwise remain difficult to access.

A reference to an institution or archive does not imply an official relationship, partnership, or endorsement.

## 5. Scientific and Academic Communities

The scientific dimension of Verse & Variable draws upon intellectual developments across mathematics, mechanics, physics, materials science, computational modelling, engineering, and related disciplines.

We acknowledge the researchers, educators, experimentalists, theorists, engineers, and scientific communities whose work contributes to these fields.

Particular appreciation is extended to the broader research communities working in areas such as:

- Nonlinear dynamics and nonlinear vibrations.
- Nonlinear elastic waves and nonlinear ultrasonics.
- Harmonic generation, scattering, and wave mixing.
- Hysteresis and contact mechanics.
- Mechanical and phononic metamaterials.
- Nonlinear optics and time-dependent physical systems.
- Computational mechanics and numerical simulation.
- Inverse problems and inverse design.
- Structural health monitoring and nondestructive evaluation.
- Physical acoustics and wave propagation.

Scientific ideas presented in the publication should be attributed to appropriate original or authoritative sources whenever possible.

Acknowledgement of a research field does not imply that researchers or institutions in that field have reviewed, validated, or endorsed the publication's interpretations.

## 6. Open-Source Software and Technical Infrastructure

Verse & Variable benefits from the work of people and communities who develop and maintain open-source software, programming languages, web standards, and digital publishing infrastructure.

The publication's technical foundation includes GitHub Pages and Jekyll, together with the wider web technologies on which the site depends.

We recognise the contributions of:

- Open-source maintainers and developers.
- Software documentation authors.
- Web standards organisations.
- Accessibility advocates.
- Browser developers.
- Typography and font designers.
- Digital publishing communities.
- Individuals who report bugs, improve documentation, and maintain shared tools.

Specific software packages, fonts, libraries, icons, and external assets should be credited in the relevant technical or asset documentation, together with their applicable licences and attribution requirements.

This acknowledgement does not replace the individual licence obligations of third-party components.

## 7. Readers and the Wider Literary Community

Readers are an important part of a publication's intellectual life.

Those who engage critically with an article, identify a translation issue, suggest a missing attribution, provide historical context, or point out a scientific inaccuracy contribute to the continuing improvement of the archive.

Verse & Variable welcomes thoughtful corrections, relevant references, and constructive criticism.

Where a reader makes a substantive contribution that merits public recognition, acknowledgement may be provided with their permission.

No reader should be publicly identified merely because they submitted private feedback or communicated with the editor.

## 8. Independent Creation and Editorial Responsibility

Verse & Variable is an independently developed literary and intellectual publication.

Its editorial direction, article selection, interpretations, and publication decisions remain independent unless a specific collaboration or other formal arrangement is explicitly documented.

Acknowledgements are not intended to suggest institutional backing, research sponsorship, financial support, or endorsement where none has been established.

The inclusion of a person, institution, publication, or software project in this document should be understood only in the context in which that contribution is described.

## 9. Accuracy and Recognition

Acknowledgements should be based on verifiable information and should recognise contributions fairly.

The publication aims to:

- Avoid invented or unsupported acknowledgements.
- Identify the nature of a contribution accurately.
- Respect the preferences of contributors regarding public recognition.
- Distinguish financial support from intellectual or technical contributions.
- Identify third-party resources and applicable licences.
- Correct acknowledgement errors when reliable information becomes available.
- Avoid implying endorsement or affiliation without evidence.

Recognition should never be used to create a misleading impression of institutional support or professional validation.

## 10. Future Contributions

As the archive grows, this page may be expanded to recognise specific individuals, institutions, archives, research communities, translators, technical contributors, and other sources of meaningful support.

Specific acknowledgements will be added when the contribution is identifiable and the wording can accurately describe its nature.

The absence of a name from this page should not be interpreted as a judgement about the importance of that person's work or contribution.

## 11. A Continuing Expression of Gratitude

The intellectual landscape explored by Verse & Variable is much larger than any single publication.

Every language carries the labour of generations. Every literary tradition preserves voices that deserve to be encountered in their original richness. Every scientific discipline advances through accumulated questioning, evidence, criticism, and collaboration.

This archive is one small contribution to that continuing exchange of ideas.

We remain grateful to the creators, preservers, researchers, translators, educators, developers, and readers whose work makes such exploration possible.
