A NOTE ON MEASUREMENT

Readership is not a measure of literary worth.

These statistics are intended to help understand how the journal is discovered and read. They are not a ranking of literary quality, intellectual significance, or cultural importance.

A page view does not represent a verified individual reader. Repeated visits and page refreshes may contribute to the aggregate count. The dashboard therefore reports readership activity without attempting to identify or profile readers.
