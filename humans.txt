/* ABOUT */

Verse & Variable is an independent literary and scientific journal.

The publication explores connections between Indian and South Asian literary traditions, poetry, shayari, philosophy, human experience and scientific thought.

Its editorial approach places literary expression first and welcomes meaningful interdisciplinary reflection.

/* LITERARY SCOPE */

Primary Languages:
Urdu, Hindi, Marathi, Kannada, Tamil, Malayalam and Telugu.

Literary Forms:
Ghazal, nazm, shayari, poetry, couplets, philosophical writing, literary passages and song lyrics.

/* SCIENTIFIC INTERESTS */

The scientific perspective includes:

- Nonlinear vibrations
- Nonlinear elastic waves
- Nonlinear ultrasonics
- Harmonic generation and scattering
- Hysteresis and contact nonlinearity
- Wave mixing
- Mechanical and phononic metamaterials
- Nonlinear optics
- Inverse problems and inverse design
- Computational mechanics
- Physical acoustics
