This Privacy Policy explains how information may be processed when visitors access Verse & Variable.

The publication is designed as a literary and intellectual archive. Respect for readers, contributors, authors, translators, and the wider literary community includes transparency about privacy.

## 1. Nature of the Website

Verse & Variable is a static publication hosted through GitHub Pages.

The website does not currently require visitors to create accounts, sign in, or submit personal information to read its articles and other publicly available materials.

The publication does not intentionally collect personal information through visitor registration forms or user-profile systems.

## 2. Hosting and Technical Information

The website is hosted through GitHub Pages. When a visitor accesses the website, GitHub and its underlying infrastructure may process certain technical information necessary for delivering, maintaining, and securing the service.

Depending on the hosting provider's practices, this may include:

- Internet Protocol (IP) address.
- Browser and device information.
- Requested pages and resources.
- Date and time of requests.
- Technical and security-related request information.

Such processing is governed by the relevant hosting provider's policies and practices.

For further information, visitors may consult the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement).

Verse & Variable does not control the hosting provider's infrastructure, internal logging practices, or data-retention periods.

## 3. External Services and Fonts

The website may load typography, fonts, or other resources from external providers.

For example, externally hosted Google Fonts may be requested from Google's servers when a page is opened. Such requests can expose technical information, including the visitor's IP address and browser-related information, to the relevant provider.

Visitors can consult Google's privacy documentation for further information:

- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [Google Fonts FAQ](https://developers.google.com/fonts/faq/privacy)

The website may also contain links to external websites, academic profiles, social platforms, publications, and other resources.

Once a visitor follows an external link, the privacy policy and data-handling practices of that destination apply.

## 4. Cookies, Analytics, and Tracking

The publication is not designed around advertising, behavioural profiling, or commercial visitor tracking.

It does not intentionally incorporate advertising trackers or tracking pixels as part of its current design.

Certain browser-side functionality, such as remembering a display preference, may use local browser storage where implemented. Such information remains within the visitor's browser environment unless a separate service is involved.

The technical behaviour of the hosting platform and external services remains subject to their respective policies.

## 5. Information Voluntarily Shared

Visitors who contact the publication's editor through an external website, email service, social platform, or other communication channel may voluntarily provide personal information.

Any information shared through those channels is handled according to the privacy practices of the relevant service and the circumstances of the communication.

Visitors should avoid sending passwords, financial information, government identification numbers, or other highly sensitive information through ordinary public communication channels.

## 6. Contributors and Literary Submissions

Contributors who submit literary material, translations, commentary, corrections, or other contributions may provide information necessary for attribution and editorial communication.

Depending on the contributor's instructions and the publication's editorial requirements, this may include:

- Preferred author or contributor name.
- Literary or academic affiliation, if voluntarily supplied.
- Language and regional identification relevant to the work.
- Bibliographic and attribution information.
- A public profile or publication link, if voluntarily supplied.

No contributor should be publicly identified, credited, or associated with a work in a manner that contradicts an explicitly communicated attribution preference.

Publication of a contribution and public attribution are editorial matters distinct from the technical processing of personal information.

## 7. Children's Privacy

The publication is not designed to collect personal information from children through accounts, registration systems, or interactive submission forms.

Parents and guardians who believe that a child has inadvertently shared personal information through a communication channel associated with the publication may contact the editor through the official website.

## 8. Data Security and Limitations

Reasonable care will be taken in managing information voluntarily received by the publication.

However, no website, hosting platform, internet transmission, or electronic communication system can guarantee absolute security.

Verse & Variable cannot guarantee the security, availability, or data-handling practices of third-party platforms and services.

## 9. External Links and Intellectual Resources

Articles may link to literary archives, academic publications, libraries, research repositories, cultural institutions, and other external resources.

These links are provided for scholarly, literary, educational, or informational purposes.

The inclusion of an external link does not mean that Verse & Variable controls, endorses, or assumes responsibility for the privacy practices of that external destination.

## 10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be revised as the publication develops, introduces new features, or changes its technical infrastructure.

Any revised version will be published on this page.

Visitors are encouraged to consult this page periodically.
