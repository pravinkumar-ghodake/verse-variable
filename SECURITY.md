
# Security Policy

## Verse & Variable

**Publication:** Verse & Variable  
**Founder and Editor:** Pravinkumar Ghodake  
**Website:** https://pravinkumar-ghodake.github.io/verse-variable/  
**Repository:** https://github.com/pravinkumar-ghodake/verse-variable/

---

## 1. Purpose

Verse & Variable takes the security, integrity and availability of its website and associated repository seriously.

This policy establishes a responsible approach to identifying, reporting and addressing potential security vulnerabilities.

The objective is to protect the publication, its contributors, its readers and the integrity of its content.

## 2. Scope

Security reports may concern:

- Website vulnerabilities.
- Cross-site scripting (XSS).
- Unsafe JavaScript behaviour.
- Exposed credentials or access tokens.
- Unintended disclosure of private information.
- Malicious or unauthorised repository changes.
- Dependency vulnerabilities.
- Unsafe third-party integrations.
- Misconfigured website security settings.
- Other technical weaknesses affecting the publication.

The policy covers the public website and its associated source repository.

## 3. Reporting a Vulnerability

Please report suspected security vulnerabilities privately rather than publishing technical details in a public issue.

The preferred contact route is the founder's official website:

https://sites.google.com/view/pravinkumarghodake

A report should ideally include:

1. A brief description of the suspected vulnerability.
2. The affected website page or repository file.
3. Steps necessary to reproduce the issue, where safe.
4. Potential impact.
5. Relevant screenshots or supporting technical information.
6. Suggested remediation, if known.

Do not include passwords, private access tokens or unrelated personal information in a report.

## 4. Responsible Disclosure

Please:

- Avoid accessing or modifying information that does not belong to you.
- Do not disrupt the availability of the website.
- Do not introduce malicious code.
- Do not conduct destructive testing.
- Do not publish sensitive vulnerability details before a reasonable opportunity for remediation.
- Avoid collecting or exposing personal information.

Security testing should remain proportionate and responsible.

## 5. Response and Remediation

Security reports will be reviewed by the publication's founder.

Where a vulnerability is confirmed, reasonable steps may be taken to:

- Assess its severity and potential impact.
- Identify affected files or services.
- Remove exposed credentials.
- Correct vulnerable code or configuration.
- Update affected dependencies.
- Restore the integrity of affected content.
- Document relevant remediation.

Response times may vary depending on the nature and complexity of the issue.

## 6. Supported Versions

The current published version of Verse & Variable is the primary version considered for security review.

Older repository states may not receive independent security maintenance.

## 7. Third-Party Services

The publication may depend on services and technologies operated by third parties, including:

- GitHub.
- GitHub Pages.
- External font providers.
- Other externally hosted resources.

Security issues within third-party infrastructure should also be reported through the relevant provider's security reporting process.

## 8. Security and Privacy Principles

The publication aims to:

- Avoid publishing credentials or confidential information.
- Minimise unnecessary collection of personal information.
- Use HTTPS for public website access.
- Keep website dependencies and configurations reasonably maintained.
- Preserve the integrity of editorial material.
- Avoid unnecessary external scripts and integrations.

## 9. Recognition

Responsible vulnerability reports are appreciated.

Recognition or public acknowledgement may be considered where appropriate and with the reporter's consent.

There is no guaranteed financial reward or bug bounty programme associated with this policy.

## 10. Policy Changes

This security policy may be updated as the website and its technical infrastructure evolve.
